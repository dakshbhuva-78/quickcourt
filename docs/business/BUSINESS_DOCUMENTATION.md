## 1. Problem statement
Players who want to play badminton, football, tennis or cricket in a city like Ahmedabad face a messy process: finding venues, phoning to ask about free hours, negotiating prices, and then coordinating friends. Venue owners, on the other hand, track bookings on paper/WhatsApp and see empty slots in off-peak hours.
| Problem | Who feels it | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| Hard to discover nearby facilities | Players | 🟠 team observation — validate by survey |
| No live view of availability / prices | Players | 🟠 |
| Group coordination takes many calls and chats | Players, groups | 🟠 |
| Unused slots, manual booking records, no-shows | Venue owners | 🟠 |
**Jobs-to-be-done:** "When I want to play this evening, help me find and secure a court within minutes" (player) · "When my courts are empty, help me fill them without extra work" (owner).
**Validation plan (do this in the project week):** (1) 30-response Google Form to students/young professionals (how they book now, pain points, willingness to pay), (2) interview 5 local venue owners, (3) record results in the report and update the assumptions below.

## 9. Revenue model
| Stream | How it works | Starting assumption (🟠) |
|---|---|---|
| Booking commission | % of each confirmed online booking, paid by venue (or added as a small convenience fee shown clearly) | 8–10 % |
| Venue-owner subscription | Monthly plan: Free (basic listing) / Pro (analytics, multiple courts, priority support) | Free + Pro tier priced after owner interviews |
| Promoted listings | Venue pays for top placement in search | Pay per week/month |
| Event promotion | Fee to promote tournaments, coaching camps, leagues | Per event |
**Illustrative unit economics (🟠):** average booking ₹500 × 10 % = ₹50 commission per booking (used in the pitch deck chart). Break-even in the pitch deck is a simple illustration, **not a forecast**.

## 10. Pricing strategy
- **Players:** no subscription; pay the venue's hourly price (shown before payment). Any convenience fee must be displayed up-front (no hidden fees → avoids dark patterns).
- **Owners:** free to list (removes adoption barrier) → commission on online bookings → optional paid Pro plan and promotions.
- **Introductory offers:** zero commission for the first 3 months for early venues (🟠 to test willingness to join).
- **Review prices** after the pilot using real data on fill rates and commission acceptance.

## 18. E-commerce design (platform flow, payment, delivery)
**Platform type:** two-sided marketplace for a *service* (time slots), not physical goods.
**Order flow:** Search → Venue details → Select court/date/slot → Booking summary (price shown) → Payment → Confirmation → Reminder → Play → Completed / Cancel & refund.
**Payment approach:** MVP = simulated UPI/Card/Cash with PENDING/SUCCESS/FAILED states and a transaction reference (no real money, no card data stored). Production = licensed gateway in **test mode first** (Razorpay/Cashfree/PayU), gateway-hosted payment page (so card data never touches our server), webhooks for payment confirmation, settlement to venue minus commission, refund API for cancellations.
**Logistics / delivery plan:** no physical delivery. "Fulfilment" = instant digital confirmation (booking ID + reference), reminders, and the venue honouring the slot. Operational risks: venue not honouring slot → complaint flow + refund; no-shows → cancellation window and (future) deposit.
**Cancellation & refund policy (suggested 🟠):** free cancellation until the slot start in MVP; production: free until X hours before, partial after; refunds to the original payment method within gateway timelines.
